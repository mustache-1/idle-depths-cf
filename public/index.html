    Support

    How to play, the rules, and how to reach the developer

    The short version

    You owe Lord Ashcombe $50M. Tap the mine to swing your pick. Cash buys pickaxes (hit harder) and crew (earn every second, even while you're away). Save up to descend — every layer pays more. Pay the debt to walk out, then go back down with lantern oil for a permanent bonus.

    Things worth knowing

    • Streaks — tap fast for up to +50% per swing. During an auto shift your streak stays maxed, and tapping along stacks an extra overtime bonus up to +100%.
    • Contracts refresh every 6 hours. Progress counts while you're away.
    • Stories unlock per layer and earn keepsakes. Mining uncovers clues.
    • The Deep Wyrm surfaces at random. Kill it in 30s for a 5-minute ×2 pick.
    • Auto-swinger — for $10M, a hired hand works the pick for you, any time. Choose 3h (3 swings/sec), 6h (2/sec), 9h (1.5/sec), or 12h (1/sec). It runs even with the game closed.
    • Offline — crew works at half pace for up to 8h (more with a better bunk).
    • The Warden's wheel — spin for up to 50×. Stake up to 25% of your cash, about a second between spins. The house keeps a small cut, so it's a thrill, not an income.

    Fair play

    Idle Depths has a shared leaderboard, so the rules are simple:

    • No automation. Autoclickers, macros, scripts, or anything that taps for you.
    • No tampering. Editing saves, memory, requests, or the page to change your numbers.
    • No exploiting bugs. If something pays out wrong, stop and report it. Fixed bugs may roll back gains.
    • One account per person. Don't run alts to farm the weekly board.
    • Keep names clean. Display names that are offensive, impersonate others, or contain links will be changed.

    Breaking these can mean removal from the leaderboard, a reset, or a ban. Calls are made by the developer and are final. Play straight and none of this matters.

    Privacy

    What we store: when you sign in with Discord we keep your Discord user ID and display name, and your game save (cash, upgrades, progress, the display name and colors you choose). That's it — no email, no messages, no friends list. The Discord login only asks for the identify scope.

    Where it lives: on Cloudflare's servers. A signed cookie keeps you logged in for 90 days.

    What's public: your display name, title, avatar colors, keepsake count, depth, oil, and lifetime cash appear on the leaderboard for anyone playing.

    Not signed in? Your save stays in your browser's local storage and never leaves your device.

    Deleting your data: use "Reset progress" in the Bunkhouse to wipe your save, or email us to remove your account entirely.

    No ads, no tracking, no selling data.
