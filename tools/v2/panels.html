The story so far

You paid Ashcombe. The ledger said PAID IN FULL and the whole mine went quiet. Then the Warm Dark sent its own bill.

Ashcombe was never the creditor. He was a collector, and the debt was always owed to something under the Throne. It settles up once a season. Each time, it reads out new Terms, four Guilds fight for the galleries, and anyone who can't pay is written into the wall.

You're not working off a debt anymore. You're deciding what the mine owes back.
